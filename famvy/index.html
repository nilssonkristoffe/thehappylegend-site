From payday to payday.

Famvy counts your month the way you live it — from the day the salary lands, not from the first. What’s left is one glance away: on the Today view, in the widget, on the lock screen and on Apple Watch. On payday, flip through the month in cards and share the last one with the family.

See where the money goes.

Income, spending and savings for the period, with a breakdown by category. Tap a category to see the purchases behind the number. Compare with the last period, and see whether the pace lands you above or below.

Every bill and subscription, together.

The Binder holds the fixed costs, subscriptions, contracts and insurance — tied to the person they belong to. Fixed costs book themselves on their due day, and the receipt or the contract can be attached right on the entry. It all stays on your device.

The routines that slip your mind.

Smoke alarm batteries, the air filter, the shower drain. Give them a rhythm and Famvy reminds you, with a Sunday check-in. Tick them off in the app — or straight in the widget.

Loans, in one figure.

Mortgage and car loan with what they cost each month and what is left. Net worth counts the accounts, the house, the car and the loans into a single number.

Six themes. The whole app changes.

Pick a colour world and everything follows — the views, the cards, the widget. Dark or light, calm either way.

What it doesn’t do.

  • No bank connection. Ever.Famvy never asks for your online-banking login. Export a file from your bank, or paste the rows — correct a row once and it comes out right next time. Your credentials are never near us, and Famvy can’t break because a bank changed its login page.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data.Your figures live on your device. The account exists to keep your iPhone, iPad, Watch and the web showing the same numbers — encrypted, in the EU.
  • It doesn’t pretend the first evening is five minutes.Entering the fixed costs, contracts and loans takes an evening. After that, five minutes a month is enough.

Questions.

Does it need my bank login?

No. Choose the file your bank already exports, or paste the rows from your statement. Transfers between your own accounts don’t count as income or spending, and loan payments get their own line.

Can my partner see the same numbers?

Yes. Invite them to the household and you both see the same whole, on your own devices. One subscription covers the family through Apple Family Sharing.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your figures stay readable. Export and account deletion always work — in front of the paywall and behind it.

Which languages and countries?

English, Swedish, German and Spanish, in your own currency from the first launch, in 175 countries. Swedish amortisation rules show up only in Sweden.
