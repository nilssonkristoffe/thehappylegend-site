Famvy

Family finance for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Reads a file from your bank — never connects to it.

Download on the App Store

Subscription. First month free.

What it does

Budget, bills and savings goals for the whole household, on one screen.

Import transactions from a file your bank already gives you. Because Famvy never connects to your bank, it works the same in 175 countries and never breaks when a bank changes its login.

Share a household with your partner. Widgets and an Apple Watch app for the numbers you check most.

What it doesn’t

No bank connection, by design.

No ads, no data trading, no selling of anything to anyone.

Your data is yours: export it, or delete your account and everything with it.
