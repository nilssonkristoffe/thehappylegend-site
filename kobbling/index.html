What it doesn’t do.

  • It is not medical care.If you drink a lot every day, stopping suddenly can be dangerous. Talk to a doctor or nurse first, so you can cut down safely. The app lists helplines for your country; in an emergency, call your local emergency number.
  • No account, no cloud, no tracking.No email and no sign-in. Your days and notes stay on your iPhone and watch — we have no server that receives them. No ads.
  • It never gives you away.Notifications never mention what you’re taking a break from.

Questions.

What can I take a break from?

Alcohol, cigarettes, vaping, nicotine pouches and snus. Choose a break with an end date, like 30 days, or stop with no end date. You can extend whenever you like.

What happens if I have a bad day?

The lantern goes out and the streak starts over. Your island keeps every piece, your badges stay, and coming back the next day earns a badge of its own.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Answer the day with a tap, breathe through a wave with gentle haptics, and see your days on the watch face. The watch gets its own copy from your phone — no account and no cloud.

Which languages?

English and Swedish. The app follows your iPhone’s language.
