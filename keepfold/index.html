Keepfold

The registration number, the Wi‑Fi password, the booking reference. Saved as cards, found in a second, copied with one tap.

Download on the App Store

Free to start. Keepfold Plus adds iCloud sync and more.

What it does

Save a card in seconds. Search it, copy it, or show it large enough to read across a counter.

Lock the sensitive ones behind Face ID. Recently deleted keeps thirty days of safety net.

Widgets for the cards you use most, and iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad with Plus — encrypted so that neither we nor Apple can read your cards.

What it doesn’t

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn sync on, and you can turn it off again and take everything with you.

No account, no ads, no tracking.

It is not a password manager, and does not pretend to be one.
