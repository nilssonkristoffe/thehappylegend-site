Keepfold for iPhone and iPad

The things you need for now.

The room number. The booking reference. The Wi‑Fi at the cabin. Keepfold keeps the small things exactly as you typed them — and tidies them away on the day you choose.

Download on the App Store

Free to start. Keepfold Plus for sync and unlimited cards.

Exactly as you typed it.

Leading zeros, spaces, capitals. No autocorrect, ever. A Wi‑Fi password that gets “helped” is a wrong password. Copy it with one tap, or show it large enough to read across a counter.

Keep until Sunday. Then it tidies itself away.

Give a card a last day. When the day is over it moves to the archive by itself. Nothing is deleted; one tap brings it back. Your list stays the list of things you need this week.

On your Home Screen. Only what you allow.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the cards you choose and nothing else. A widget can be seen by anyone standing next to you — so you decide, card by card.

Locked with Face ID.

A locked card shows dots until it’s you. It never appears in a widget, and search can’t find its value. A shield against a glance — not a safe, and we say so.

Your iCloud, encrypted.

Turn on sync and your iPad sees the same cards. They live in your own iCloud as an encrypted field — we can’t read them, and neither can Apple. Off by default.

What it doesn’t do.

  • No account.Nothing to sign up for. Keepfold works the second it opens.
  • No tracking, no ads.The app contains no analytics and talks to no server of ours.
  • It is not a password manager.Locks stop a glance, not an attacker. Keep real secrets in a real password manager — and the room number in Keepfold.

Questions.

Does it work without iCloud?

Yes. Sync is off by default and everything lives on your device. Turn it on if you want your iPad to see the same cards.

What happens to a card when its day is over?

It moves to the archive. Nothing is deleted. Open the archive and tap it to bring it back.

Can I get my cards out?

Yes — export from Settings, whether you have Plus or not. Your data is yours.

Which languages?

English and Swedish. The app follows your iPhone’s language.
