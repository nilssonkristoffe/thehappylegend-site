In review at Apple

Count protein,not calories.

Gramvy counts one number: grams of protein. Log in two taps on your iPhone or Apple Watch, and see what is left at a glance.

Sent to Apple for review on 9 October 2026. This page will link to the App Store the day it is out. Free to start. Gramvy Plus is $39.99 a year, with 7 days free.

Your goal, in grams.

Pick a goal in plain words and Gramvy turns it into grams for your body weight. The reference values, like 1.6 g per kilo, come with their sources. Then it shows you what a day like that can look like.

Log it the way you eat it.

Portions you actually eat, like a tub, two eggs or a glass of milk, in g, ml or dl. Nearly 200 everyday foods with protein values from USDA FoodData Central and the Swedish Food Agency.

From your wrist Plus

Tap a favourite on Apple Watch and the ring moves. Grams left sit on your watch face.

Scan or snap Plus

Scan a barcode, or photograph the nutrition label. The label is read on your iPhone, and the photo is never stored.

Just the grams Free

Already know the number? Type it. Or say "Log protein in Gramvy" to Siri.

Open Gramvy on your watch, tap a favourite. That is the whole log, between sets or on the way out of the kitchen.

When the day falls short.

Fill the gap Plus

Gramvy suggests up to three foods you already eat, with the amount that closes the gap, rounded up to the nearest 5 g. One tap logs it, on your iPhone or your Apple Watch.

An evening reminder Free

At a time you choose, and only on days you have not reached your goal. No streaks, no guilt. It is off until you turn it on.
