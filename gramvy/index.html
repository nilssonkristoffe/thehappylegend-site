Gramvy

Count protein, not calories.

A protein tracker for people who lift. Log in two taps on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Scan a barcode, photograph a nutrition label, or pick from a list of everyday foods with sourced protein values. No account. Your data stays on your devices.
