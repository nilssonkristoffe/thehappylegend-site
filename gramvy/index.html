Gramvy

Count protein, not calories.

A protein tracker for people who lift. Log in two taps on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Scan a barcode, photograph a nutrition label, or pick from nearly 200 everyday foods with sourced protein values. No account. Your log stays on your devices.

What it does

  • Pick your goal in plain words. Gramvy shows it in grams for your body weight, with the reference values and their sources.
  • Log your favourites from Apple Watch in two taps, and see grams left on your watch face. Plus
  • Fill the gap: when you are short, Gramvy suggests foods you already eat, with the amount that closes it. One tap logs it. Plus
  • An evening reminder at a time you choose, only on days you have not reached your goal. Off until you turn it on.
  • Writes your protein to Apple Health, if you want it to.

Free and Plus

Free, for good: Just the grams, three saved foods, the ring, the last 7 days, widgets, Apple Health, Siri and the evening reminder. Gramvy Plus adds food search, barcode and label scanning, unlimited saved foods, the Apple Watch app, Fill the gap, full history and the weight trend. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. Prices for your country are shown in the App Store and in the app before you buy.
