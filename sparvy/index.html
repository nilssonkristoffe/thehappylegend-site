Sparvy

One sheet per question. Drag a number and watch the answer change while you drag.

Download on the App Store

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

Save: monthly amount, years, return — and what fees actually cost you, in money and in share.

Borrow: mortgage, car or personal loan. Monthly cost, total interest, extra payments, split rates.

Pay: what is left after tax, with the real Swedish tax rules for the current year — municipal rate, state tax, the earned-income credit, company car.

What it doesn’t

No account. No server. Nothing leaves your phone.

It never predicts markets or recommends products. The rate you type is yours.

No ads, no subscription, nothing more to buy.
