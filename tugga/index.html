How it works

Three ways to log. One tap to see where you stand.

01

Search or scan

The Swedish Food Agency list and Swedish store products, right on your phone. The barcode is usually enough.

02

Snap the plate

The camera reads the nutrition label or your plate, you confirm. Everything is computed on your phone.

03

Say what you ate

"Two eggs and a slice of bread." Done. Speech is recognised on the device.

Thought through

Everything you are used to from the big apps. None of the noise.

We tried a great many calorie apps. Each did something well, and each drowned in the rest: streaks, points, badges, praise and reminders nobody asked for. In the end we got tired of it and built our own. We kept what actually helps and left the rest.

Habits

Seven rings, one a week. Tick off what you decided on. No chain to break, no shame when a day stays empty.

Caffeine

Coffee and tea logged with one tap. The curve shows what is left in your body tonight, and when the last cup should be.

Weight

The trend instead of today's number. Goal weight as a line, milestones when they happen. BMI if you want it, never if you don't.

Daily receipt

Your day as a receipt. Share it as an image if you like.

Eating out

Pick the dish and the size, get an honest range instead of a made-up number.

My meals

Save breakfast once. Then one tap.

Watch and widget

The ring on your wrist and your home screen. Nothing more.

If you train

Macros in grams. Workouts count. Nobody cheering.

Protein, carbs and fat as targets you set yourself. Workouts from Apple Health are added to the ring if you want. The weight trend is shown as it is, without praise and without warnings. Choose lose, maintain or build, and the target follows.

Questions

Is there a free version?

Yes. Search, barcode, voice, your own foods, the ring, meal cards and weight are free with no time limit. Pro adds nutrition targets, habits, caffeine, weight goals in depth, the camera after five scans, Watch and widgets.

I live outside Sweden. Will the food work?

Staple foods (fruit, vegetables, meat, grains, dairy) are available in Swedish, English, German, Spanish and French. Barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts, which covers most of Europe well. Store products outside Sweden are not in the built-in library yet, but you can scan the label and save the product in two seconds. We expand country by country and always say which countries are done.

Do I need an account?

No. Download and start. Your food is stored on your phone and in your iCloud.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. What you logged stays, and the free part works as before. The Pro parts pause until you choose Pro.
