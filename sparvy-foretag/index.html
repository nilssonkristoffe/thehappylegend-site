The handbook in the company’s everyday.

Salary or dividend? What does the car cost the company? How much VAT, and which way? When is the F-tax due? Five pages that answer in kronor, on this year’s rules, with a source on every number. In Swedish, for Sweden.

Salary or dividend? The answer in kronor.

Enter the year’s profit and see what ends up in your wallet after employer contributions, corporate tax and the 3:12 rules — the threshold amount, what is taxed at 20 % and what goes above it. Tap “Best split” and Sparvy Företag shows the two honest answers: the most in the wallet now, or a full public pension.

VAT both ways.

Type a price excluding VAT and see it including — or the other way round, with one tap. 25, 12 or 6 %, a list of what falls under which rate, and the VAT period that applies to you.

The car on the company. What it costs.

Leasing, insurance, service, the mileage — and the benefit value from the new-car price, on the 2026 formula. Electric, hybrid or petrol. Compare with owning the car yourself.

The pension. Three roads, one answer.

Take the money as an occupational pension, as salary, or as dividend. Sparvy Företag shows what each road gives after tax in twenty years — the same money, three ways.

The dates that apply.

F-tax, the VAT quarter, the residual tax — the next twelve months of deadlines for your kind of company, with a reminder the day before.

The handbook is included.

Every page has a plain explanation written for the person who just started a company: how to fill it in, what the number is, and why it matters.

Questions.

Does it cover enskild firma?

Yes. Switch between aktiebolag and enskild firma on the page, and the calculation changes with it — egenavgifter and income tax instead of employer contributions and dividend.

What happens when the rules change in January?

The app is updated with the new amounts and percentages, and the update is included in the price.

Is the benefit value right?

It follows Skatteverket’s 2026 formula — the base amount, the interest part and the price part of the new-car price — and was checked against Skatteverket’s own examples.

Can I deduct the app?

Ask your accountant — that is exactly the kind of question the handbook is for.
