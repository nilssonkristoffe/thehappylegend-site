Sparvy Företag

The handbook for running an aktiebolag or enskild firma. Five sheets, this year’s rules, sources on every number.

Download on the App Store

One-time purchase. Sweden only, in Swedish.

What it does

VAT both ways. Salary or dividend — and what the difference costs you in pension. The car on the company. Pension. Every deadline of the year, with a reminder the day before.

Calculated on the 2026 rules, including the 3:12 changes from 1 January.

A built-in handbook that explains every line for the first-time company owner.

What it doesn’t

It gives no advice. It shows what happens, with a source on every figure, so you can walk into your accountant with the right question.

No account, no server, nothing leaves your phone.

No ads, no subscription.
